!!⚠️ Template vs Strategy!!
Template
   * Inheritance-based
   * Fixed algorithm structure
Strategy
   * Composition-based
   * Replace behavior entirely

Template = "steps are fixed, details vary"
Strategy = "behavior itself varies"

🔥 When to Use Template ?
When:
    You have a fixed workflow with small variations
