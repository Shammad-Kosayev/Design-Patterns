The Core Idea
Observer =3
    "when one thing changes, notify others automatically"

Real-Life Analogy
    YouTube Channel → Subscribers
    Channel uploads video
    Subscribers get notified
    👉 Channel = Subject
    👉 Subscribers = Observers

Advanced Insight (IMPORTANT)
Observer has 2 styles:
    🔹 Push Model
        observer.onOrderPlaced(orderId);
    👉 Subject sends data
    🔹 Pull Model
        observer.update(this);
    👉 Observer pulls what it needs

🔥 11️⃣ Problems of Observer
Be aware:
    ❌ debugging can be hard
    ❌ hidden side effects
    ❌ execution order unclear
    ❌ can become chaotic

🔥 7️⃣ Real Backend Examples
1️⃣ Spring Events
    In Spring Framework:

    @EventListener

    }
