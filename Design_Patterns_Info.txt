| What Are Design Patterns? |
└---------------------------┘
Design Patterns =
    "reusable solutions to common software design problems"

👉 Not code.
👉 Not frameworks.
👉 Not libraries.

They are:
    "structured ways of solving recurring design problems"

Design patterns are NOT ready-to-use code ❌
Design patterns are reusable IDEAS ✅

┌-------------------------------┐---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Why Do Design Patterns Exist? |
└-------------------------------┘
Because developers kept facing the same problems:
❌ Without Design Patterns
    - duplicated logic
    - tight coupling
    - messy if-else chains
    - hard-to-extend systems
    - fragile codebases

✅ With Design Patterns
    - cleaner structure
    - better separation of concerns
    - easier extensibility
    - reusable architecture

🔥Key Idea
    Same problems appear again and again → patterns are the answers

┌--------------------------------------┐--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| What Kind of Problems Do They Solve? |
└--------------------------------------┘
❌ NOT This
    “How do I calculate discount?”
    “How do I sort a list?”

👉 algorithm problem, not design

✅ THIS
    “How do I change behavior dynamically?”
    “How do I avoid creating too many objects?”
    “How do I structure complex object relationships?”

👉 design problem → pattern applies

┌-----------------------------------------┐-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Why Are They Divided Into 3 Categories? |
└-----------------------------------------┘
Every system has 3 fundamental concerns:
    1️⃣ Creation
        "How do objects come into existence?"
    2️⃣ Structure
        "How are they connected?"
    3️⃣ Behavior
        "How do they act and interact?"

The classic classification comes from the book:
    👉 Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software

🏗️ 1. Creational Patterns:
   * Singleton
   * Factory Method
   * Abstract Factory
   * Builder

🧱 2. Structural Patterns
   * Adapter
   * Bridge
   * Composite
   * Decorator
   * Facade
   * Proxy

🔁 3. Behavioral Patterns
   * Strategy
   * Observer
   * Command
   * Chain of Responsibility
   * Template Method
   * Visitor

┌------------------------------------┐----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| 🚫 When NOT to Use Design Patterns |
└------------------------------------┘

❌ Simple codebase
    No need for abstraction
❌ One-time logic
❌ No scalability requirement
❌ You don’t understand the problem

┌--------------------------------------------------┐--------------------------------------------------------------------
| 🧠 What You Should Know Before Learning Patterns |
└--------------------------------------------------┘
You need:
✅ OOP Fundamentals
   * abstraction
   * inheritance
   * polymorphism
   * encapsulation

✅ SOLID Principles
Especially:
   * Open/Closed
   * Single Responsibility

✅ Real Problem Awareness
    You must feel the pain first
