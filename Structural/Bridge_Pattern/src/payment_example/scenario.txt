🎯 Scenario: Payment System
You support:
    Payment types:
        * One-time payment
        * Subscription payment
    Payment providers:
        * Stripe
        * PayPal

🔴 What’s the problem?
Right now:
    2 types × 2 providers = 4 classes

Now imagine adding:
    * ApplePay
    * GooglePay
💥 You get:
    2 × 4 = 8 classes
Add more types?
    N × M explosion

🧠 2️⃣ WHY This Happens
Because you're mixing two independent dimensions:
    * Dimension 1 → Payment type (what)
    * Dimension 2 → Provider (how)

Bridge Solution (Step-by-step)
We separate those dimensions.

🔥 What Bridge Will Fix (Preview)
Instead of:
    SubscriptionStripePayment ❌
You’ll have:
    SubscriptionPayment + StripeProvider ✅
