🔴 WHY THIS IS BAD

❌ 1. Telescoping Constructor
    new Coffee(true, false, true, false)
    👉 nobody can read these arguments at the call site ❌

❌ 2. Add-On Logic Spread Across Two Methods
    cost()     has its own pile of if's ❌
    describe() has its own pile of if's ❌
    Adding "soy milk" = touching BOTH methods + the constructor.
    Easy to forget one of them → silent bug 💀

❌ 3. Cannot Combine Freely
    "Espresso with double sugar"?
        boolean addSugar can only be true once ❌
    Real customers DO order double sugar. Real life broke your model.

❌ 4. Cannot Vary The Base Drink
    Whole class assumes "Espresso".
    Want a Latte with milk + caramel?
        → new class? new constructor?
        → another telescoping constructor in another class? 💀

❌ 5. Violates Open/Closed Principle
    Add new add-on → MODIFY existing class.
    Every change risks breaking existing orders.

❌ 6. Hard To Test
    cost() depends on 4 booleans → 16 combinations to verify ❌
    Add a 5th add-on → 32. Then 64. Then 128.

❌ 7. Add-Ons Are Not Reusable
    "Caramel costs $0.7" lives ONLY inside this class.
    Cannot reuse the caramel rule for the Latte class.
